Mintline - how to open

Double-click "Open Mintline.bat".
Mintline opens in its own window (using Chrome if you have it,
otherwise your default browser).

Want an icon on your desktop?
Right-click "Open Mintline.bat" > Show more options > Send to > Desktop (create shortcut).
You can rename that shortcut to "Mintline".

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" then "Run anyway".
It only opens index.html from this folder.

Keep all the files in this folder together.
